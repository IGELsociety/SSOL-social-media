## The complete SSOL archive, 2011–2022

*Scientific Study of Literature* (SSOL) is the journal of the **International
Society for the Empirical Study of Literature (IGEL)**. From its first issue in
2011 until 2022, SSOL was published by **John Benjamins** as a
subscription-based journal.

The full archive of those volumes — every article from 2011 to 2022 — is
catalogued in our public
[Zotero group library](https://www.zotero.org/groups/6608170/ssol-archive-2011-2022/library).
Anyone can browse the bibliographic records, read the abstracts, and follow the
DOI of each article.

### How to read the full text

Because these volumes were published under subscription, the PDFs cannot be
posted openly. There are two ways to get them:

- **Become an IGEL member.** Members get access to the PDFs of the entire
  2011–2022 archive through the society's Zotero group library.
  [Join IGEL here](https://igelsociety.org/member/).
- **Through the publisher.** Every article remains available from
  [John Benjamins](https://www.benjamins.com/catalog/ssol), and via your
  institution's subscription if it has one.

### SSOL is now diamond open access

Since **2023**, SSOL is published as a **diamond open access** journal: free to
read for everyone, and free to publish in — no subscription fees, and no article
processing charges for authors.

Diamond open access does not happen by itself; it is sustained by the society
behind the journal. If you value a literature journal that charges neither
readers nor authors, the most direct way to support it is to
[become an IGEL member](https://igelsociety.org/member/). Membership keeps SSOL
independent, keeps it free, and gives you access to the full historical archive
at the same time.
